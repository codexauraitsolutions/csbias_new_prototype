The Degree + IAS Integrated Program is designed for students who have just completed Intermediate and are certain about a career in the Civil Services. Instead of starting UPSC preparation after graduation, you build the foundation alongside your degree — saving up to two years.

“By the time our integrated students graduate, they've already written 150+ tests.”
  • Recognised UG degree through our partner university
  • Complete GS Prelims & Mains coverage across three years
  • Daily current affairs and newspaper analysis
  • Personality development and mock interviews
Year 1 — Foundation

NCERT foundation (Class 6–12), Indian Polity, Ancient & Medieval History, Physical Geography, basic Economics, CSAT basics.

Year 2 — Core GS

Modern History, Indian & World Geography, Indian Economy, Environment, Science & Technology, Prelims test series.

Year 3 — Mains & Optional

GS Papers I–IV, Ethics case studies, Essay, optional subject, answer writing and mock interviews.
